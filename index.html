03About

I am a trade compliance practitioner with more than fifteen years inside export-manufacturing and cross-border operations in Latin America, now based in Newport News, Virginia. My formation was operational, not advisory: I learned customs valuation by defending it, free trade agreements by certifying under them, and suspensive regimes by reconciling their inventories month after month, inside plants where the consequences of a loose file arrived with interest.

The files I have carried include multi-million-dollar customs valuation audits argued through to settlement, royalty and licence-fee disputes decided on the strength of contract language, origin qualification and its defense under verification across CAFTA-DR, the EU–Central America Association Agreement and the Central American regime, and the documentary architecture behind Authorized Economic Operator certification. That operational history is the raw material of everything published on this site: I write only about what I have worked.

Today that experience is available two ways. The analyses on this page, published in professional venues and kept here in their canonical, most complete versions. And independent advisory, remote, for companies and counsel who are looking at a valuation question, an origin file, or an audit letter and want someone who has sat on the operator’s side of that table.
